📦 Understanding Firewood Volumes: Ton Bags vs. Loose Tipper Loads

In the UK firewood sector, customers are often confused by bag terminology. A standard "Ton Bag" (builders dumpy bag) is a volumetric measure holding approximately 0.7m³ to 0.85m³ of loose seasoned hardwood (weighing around 200kg–300kg, not a metric tonne!). For regular wood-burner households and country estates, our Half Transit Tipper Load (£165) (~1.3m³–1.5m³) and Full Transit Tipper Load (£330) (~2.6m³–3.0m³) deliver substantially more cubic volume loose-tipped directly onto your driveway, saving you £50–£60 compared to regional bagged prices without packaging waste.
