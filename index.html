5+ Years Supplying Locally

Quality Hardwood Firewood You Can Rely On

For over five years, H S Logs has been supplying premium seasoned firewood to homeowners, pubs, estates, and wood-fired pizza businesses across Heathfield and East Sussex. We believe great firewood comes from doing things properly without taking shortcuts.


Our wood is sourced locally from forestry commission woodlands and left to season thoroughly in long cord lengths. Once naturally conditioned, we break the timber down on our own machinery into uniform split logs tailored to fit standard wood burners, multi-fuel stoves, traditional inglenook fireplaces, and commercial pizza vans. From there, the logs go into our dry barns for natural air circulation until they are ready to deliver.


Because we process all timber ourselves, we can guarantee genuine quality: dense, clean Prime, Beech & Ash hardwood that lights easily, burns hot and long, and keeps your chimney clean.

From Woodland to Hearth

How We Produce Your Firewood

Every log delivered has been properly handled and dried through our proven 4-stage process.

1

Forestry Sourced

Harvested locally from certified Forestry Commission woodlands in Sussex, focusing on high-density Beech & Ash.

2

Cord Seasoning

Timber is rested in long cord lengths to allow initial natural seasoning and sap evaporation before splitting.

3

In-House Splitting

Processed down on our own machinery into uniform logs sized specifically for domestic stoves and open hearths.

4

Barn Air-Drying

Stacked inside well-ventilated barns to protect from rain while circulating dry air for maximum combustion efficiency.
